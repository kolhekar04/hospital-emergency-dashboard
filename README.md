# 🏥 Hospital Emergency Room Data Analytics Dashboard

An interactive Excel analytics dashboard designed to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), patient demographics, wait times, and department referral trends for a hospital emergency room across 2023 and 2024.

## 📌 Project Overview
The objective of this project is to analyze operational efficiency and patient satisfaction within the Emergency Room (ER). By tracking wait times, attendance status, and departmental loads, the dashboard enables healthcare administrators to identify bottlenecks, optimize resource allocation, and improve overall patient care quality.

### Dashboard Highlights & Key Metrics

### 🔹 High-Level KPIs (Filtered View Example: March)
* **Total Patients Attended:** 506 patients
* **Average Wait Time:** 35.26 minutes
* **Average Patient Satisfaction:** 5.33 / 10

### 🔹 Operational Efficiency & Demographics
* **Service Timeliness:** 38% of patients were attended on time, while 62% experienced delays.
* **Admission Rate:** 50.00% Admitted (253 patients) vs. 50.00% Not Admitted (253 patients).
* **Gender Distribution:** 54% Male vs. 46% Female.
* **Age Breakdown:** Highest patient inflow observed in the 30–39 (73) and 60–69 (74) age groups.
* **Department Referrals:** "None" represents the largest volume, followed by General Practice, Orthopedics, and Cardiology.

### 🔹 Daily Performance Tracking
* Detailed tracking sheets analyzing **Daily Total Patients**, **Daily Average Wait Time**, and **Daily Average Score** across the month (ranging between 1.00 and 9.25 satisfaction rating).

## 🛠️ Tools & Techniques Used
* **Microsoft Excel:** Pivot Tables, Data Formatting, Data Modeling, Dynamic Charting.
* **Data Visualization:** Custom KPIs, Donut Charts, Horizontal Bar Charts, Area Charts, Column Charts.
* **Interactive Elements:** Year (2023/2024) and Month (Jan–Dec) Slicers for seamless data filtering.
